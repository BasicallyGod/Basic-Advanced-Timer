Week overview: meetings and notes under each day

Under each day's name the chart shows how many meetings and open dated notes it holds, as a small calendar icon and note icon with a number. They float with no frame and no words, so they are easy to miss and hard to read. Five other ways to show the same, drawn on the real chart at its real width with sample days that have none, one or several. Switch Light or Dark in Tweaks. Drawings only, nothing built.

C · A dot for each

One small mark per item: a dashed square for a meeting (as drawn on the timeline) and a hollow circle for a note, six at most and then +N. It shows how busy a day is at a glance, but not what is in it.
