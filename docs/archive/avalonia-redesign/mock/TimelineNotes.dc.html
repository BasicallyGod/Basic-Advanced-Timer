Notes on the timeline, quietly

Today a note shows only in the Notes card, and a timed reminder has no place on the day. Five ways to show them on the timeline without crowding the blocks and meetings, drawn on the same day at the real 100 pixels an hour (08:30 to 17:30, the now marker at 14:15). Two notes are late, two are coming, and two have no time, since only a note with a reminder has a time to sit at. Switch Light or Dark in the Tweaks. Drawings only, nothing built.

B · A thin lane under the ruler
A 26 pixel lane holds each note as a small chip that starts at its time, so the words are readable without a hover. Passed reminders are tinted red. Notes with no time sit in one dashed +2 chip at the left, with their words in a tooltip. It costs 34 pixels of height.
