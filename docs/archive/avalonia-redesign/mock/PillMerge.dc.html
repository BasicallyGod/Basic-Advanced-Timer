Playing and switching in one place

Today the pill lets you pause or resume the account but sends you to the Accounts button, at the foot under the break, to change it. These explore putting the two together on one background, so the control that says what you are tracking is also the one that changes it. In each, the break moves to the foot. Everything else about the pill is as built: the same 72 by 316 card, ring, glow and soft tints. The rail's top is the same column, so whichever is chosen is drawn on both.

As built
Play or pause, then the break, then the Accounts button, three separate tiles.
  • Switching account is a second target under the break.
  • Nothing ties the three bars to the account being tracked.

A. One well
The two tiles keep their own tints but sit on one soft shared background, and the break moves out below it.
  • Behaviour is unchanged: the tile pauses or resumes, the bars open the list.
  • The smallest change. It says these two belong together, and the break reads as a separate thing.
  • Needs 98 pixels of height, the same as the two tiles and the gap they take now.

B. Split button
One surface in the state colour, green or red in overtime, with a hairline between the two halves.
  • The tile and the bars read as one button with two halves, like a split button.
  • The whole group takes the state colour, so overtime turns both red.
  • The bars sit on the green wash, so the account colours are a little quieter than in A.

C. Quick switch
The well also holds the last three accounts you used as dots. One click on a dot changes account, and the small bars underneath open the full list.
  • Switching is one click, with no drawer, which answers having to go somewhere else.
  • A dot shows its account name on hover. With fewer than four accounts there are fewer dots.
  • The dots are the three used most recently apart from the running one, so they change as you work.
  • The well is a little taller, so the break sits about 2 pixels lower.

What I would pick

C if changing account is the thing to fix, since it removes the trip altogether. A if you only want it to feel less scattered. B is the most striking and the least different in what it does.

Open questions

Should the break move to the foot in any of these? Should the dots be the most recent accounts or ones you pin? Do you want the dots on the rail too, where there is more room, or only on the pill?

Nothing is built

These are drawings only. Switch Light and Dark in the tweaks. The account drawer, the reminder light and the dashboard are unchanged.
