Every icon in the app

Drawn from the app's own drawing code (IconGlyph and the controls that draw their own symbols), at rest, on the 18 unit grid they are built on. Each card shows the icon large over its grid (the dashed square is a 2 unit margin), then at real size in a button, in the accent, and at the 14 and 12 pixel sizes the app also uses. The line under the name is the ink size in grid units (the pen included) and how far its centre is from the grid's middle; red means more than half a unit out. Switch Light or Dark in Tweaks.

What this sheet shows
Weight. One pen, 1.6 wide, round caps and joins, on every line icon. The deliberate exceptions are the Week baseline (1.25), the Notes list's two text lines (1.7) and Minimise's second line (1.0, an icon nothing draws).
Size. Most icons fill 13 to 15 of the 18 units. Gear is the biggest at 15.9. Close is the smallest at 10.6 across, where Plus and Minus are 12.6, so in the same bar of buttons the cross reads a size smaller than its neighbours. Check is 12.1 by 9.9, also small.
Centre. Most icons sit within a quarter unit of the middle. Bell hangs +0.75 low and Week +1.51 low (its baseline is at the bottom of the grid, the columns above it are centred on the line), so each wants a nudge wherever it is placed. Grip is a narrow strip by design and is not used.
Corners. The boxes use four radii: 2.5 (Today, Table), 2 (Copy, Dock), 1.8 (the Notes list boxes) and 1.4 to 1.5 (rail bars and columns). The set would hold together better on two.
Two languages. The rail's three icons are solid shapes at 22; every other icon is a 1.6 line. That is intended, since the rail is the quiet navigation, but Play, Pause and the Accounts bars are solid as well, so the rail is not the only solid set.
Never drawn. Five icons are defined and nothing draws them: Edit, Table, Entries, Grip and Minimise. They could go.
Drawn elsewhere. Chevron, Play and Pause, the cup, the Accounts bars, the account tiles and the two ticks each have their own drawing code and their own pen (1.6, solid, 2.0, 2.2 and 2.4). The ticks are the odd ones out: three tick marks at three weights, none of them the set's Check.
