Docked accounts: opening and closing

Option A, drawn so the move between the panel being closed and docked is smooth and reversible. Try it below, then read how it is put together.

The prototype
The Accounts button on the rail opens and closes the docked panel; the pin in the panel's heading lets it float over the page instead (and back). Switch pages on the rail to see Timeline, Notes and Week reflow, and click again in the middle of a move to turn it round. In Tweaks, Speed slows it down so each part can be watched, and Light or Dark changes the look.

One number drives all of it
Everything is a function of one eased progress value, 0 (folded away) to 1 (docked), so opening and closing are the same path run forwards and backwards, nothing is ever squeezed on the way, and a click half way simply turns it round from where it is. Here are the Timeline and the Notes page at six moments of the 280 ms opening.

What moves, in what order
Progress is the eased 0 to 1 value. Each part has its own window of it, so the page gives up room before it would be squeezed and the new layout is already in place when the panel arrives.
PartProgress windowWhat it does
The panel0 to 1Its width runs from 0 to 272, anchored at the rail, with its contents laid out at full width and clipped, so nothing in it reflows. Its edge line appears with the first pixels.
Rail Accounts button0 to 1The blue wash under the three bars comes up with the panel and goes with it.
Heading and pin.12 to .47Fade in once the panel is wide enough to hold them.
Account rows.14 to .90Each fades in and slides 12 px from the left, one after another (45 thousandths of progress apart, about 12 ms), top to bottom.
The page's room0 to 1The page is pushed by the same width as the panel, so the two edges stay together with no gap and no overlap.
Timeline bar0 to .5Unallocated and Breaks fold away (width and opacity together, with their gap) before the bar would run short of room. They stay in the bar's tooltip.
Header0 to .5The Today button folds away the same way, and the date shortens a little.
Timeline itselfalwaysKeeps its zoom and its scroll position at the left edge, so no block moves; the right end is simply uncovered or covered.
Selected block card.08 to .55Its contents fade first (0 to .4) while the card narrows to nothing and its gap closes, so the Notes card grows into the room. It is not gone: it comes back as a tab.
Selected block tab.45 to .85Opens at the left of the Notes card's switch (Selected block, Notes, Meetings). Selecting a block on the timeline switches to it by itself, and back when the selection is cleared.
Notes page: views0 to .5The views and accounts column folds away to the left.
Notes page: chips.3 to .8A row of view chips opens above the form (with Accounts as a filter), pushing the list down smoothly.
Week overviewnone neededThe chart and the By account column are already flexible: the chart narrows, By account keeps its width, and the totals strip drops Scheduled and Unallocated.
Timing and feel
Opening280 ms, easing out quickly (cubic-bezier .22, .8, .24, 1): most of the width arrives in the first 100 ms, then it settles, so the page feels pushed rather than dragged.
Closing220 ms, ease in and out (cubic-bezier .4, 0, .2, 1): shorter, because closing is dismissing, and symmetrical with opening in what moves.
Pinning and unpinning260 ms: the page eases into or out of the room while the panel stays put and gains or loses its shadow, so floating and docked are one control, not two modes.
Turning round half wayAllowed. Progress simply reverses from wherever it is, with the new direction's duration.
Reduced motionUnder Windows' Show animations off, everything jumps to its end state at once (Motion.Ease is zero), and the order above does not matter.
FocusOpening or closing never moves the keyboard focus. A note being typed stays in its box, and the panel is not modal: Escape still clears the selection, and does not close a pinned panel.
RememberedOpen or closed, and pinned or floating, are remembered between runs (one new settings line each), and a dashboard narrower than the room the panel needs shows it floating, as today.
Cost in the appThe page re-lays out for each frame of the 280 ms. The Timeline is cheap (fixed zoom, clipped), and the heavier pages are given the same width by one eased property, so there is one layout pass per frame and no per-element animation to fall out of step.
