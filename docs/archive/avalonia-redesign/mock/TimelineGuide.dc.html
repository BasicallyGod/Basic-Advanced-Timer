Pins with a guide line

Option A from the last board (a dot on the ruler at each note's time), with a dashed guide that follows the pointer along the timeline so it is clear what is being pointed at. Three states on the same day at 100 pixels an hour, then the line drawn three ways. The guide would also show on the Week and Notes views only if asked for; here it is the timeline only. Switch Light or Dark in the Tweaks. Drawings only, nothing built.

1 · Over empty time
The guide follows the pointer along the day. A small time chip rides at the top of the ruler, and whatever lies under the line, here Account two and the Client call, gets a soft ring. The pins stay as they are.

2 · Near a note: the line snaps to its pin
Within a few pixels of a pin the guide snaps to that note's time. The pin swells, the chip turns to the accent wash and the note's words appear beside the line, so what is being pointed at is the note and its moment.
