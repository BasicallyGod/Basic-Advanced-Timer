Accounts alongside the pages

Today the account list opens over the whole page behind a dim cover, so you lose the Notes form or the Timeline while you choose. Three ways to keep both in view. Switch Light or Dark in Tweaks.

A. A docked panel that pushes the page aside
The Accounts button opens the same panel, but pinned: it takes its own 272 pixels beside the rail and the page reflows into what is left (752 wide), so nothing is covered and nothing is dimmed. Pick an account, or start and stop one, and carry on typing. Unpinned, it floats over the page as it does today, which stays the choice for anyone who wants the room back.
  • The rail's Accounts button shows the open wash while the panel is docked; pressing it folds the panel away and the page takes its width back.
  • Timeline: the bar keeps Add time, Undo, Redo, Working time with its bar, Allocated and zoom; Unallocated and Breaks move into the bar's tooltip. The Selected block card and the Notes card become one card with a switch (Selected block, Notes, Meetings), so a note can be written while the panel is in view.
  • Notes: the views list across the top as chips (with an Accounts filter list), and the form wraps to two lines.

B. A slim strip that is always there, and opens into A
A 68 pixel strip of the accounts sits beside the rail: each is its colour with a ring for its budget (green on the running one) and today's time under it. It costs the page 68 pixels, so the Timeline keeps nearly all of its layout. Click an account to switch to it; hover for its name and budget; the arrow at the top expands the strip into the full docked panel of option A, and back.
  • Nothing to open or close for the common job, which is switching account while you work.
  • The running account's row is tinted, as in the list, so the strip also answers what is recording at a glance.
  • Pages need only a small reflow (956 wide): the two lower cards stay side by side, a little narrower.

C. Recording chip in the header, with the list hung beneath
No panel at all: the page header carries a chip for what is recording (the dot, the name, today's time and a chevron), and it hangs the account list from a point beneath it, the same card the horizontal pill uses. The page is untouched and fully usable around it; the list closes on a click outside or Escape. The cheapest to build, and the closest to how the horizontal pill already behaves, but the accounts are not on show all the time.

How the pages react to the room they have
One rule set for every option, driven by the width the page has rather than by which panel is open, so the same layouts also serve a narrower window if the dashboard is ever resizable.

Which to choose

A on its own is the most direct answer: the same panel, kept in view. B adds a way to switch account without opening anything and expands into A, so it covers the most; it is also the most to build. C is the least change, and fits if the account list does not need to stay in view.

What stays the same

The rail, the pill and the account rows (name, time, budget bar, play button). The panel's rows reorder by dragging as before. Manage accounts is still the sliders icon at the top right of the heading.

Questions

Should the choice (floating, docked, strip) be remembered per window, or set in Settings? Should the strip show today's time under each account, or only the initials and ring?
