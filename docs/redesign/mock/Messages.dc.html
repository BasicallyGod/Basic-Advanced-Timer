Rules
  • Say what happened in a few words, with no full stop.
  • Detail comes after a dot, in the quiet colour, and only when it helps.
  • A failed edit says what to try: "End: try 10:30", never "looks like".
  • Offer one button that names its action (Undo, Try again, Continue Account one).
  • Done is a tick on the accent. Not possible and Needs you are a ring with a mark, never red.
  • Selection is not a message: it stays in the Selected block card.
