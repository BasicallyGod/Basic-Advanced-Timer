New themes: Midnight, Hawthorn, and a second go at Slate, Sage and Mono

Midnight and Hawthorn are as first shown. Slate, Sage and Mono are redrawn after feedback. Each is shown on the real Timeline and Week layouts and on the pill in every state, with the account drawer. Every theme has its own account colours, so nothing clashes. Nothing here is built yet.

What is common to all five
  • The same layout, controls and motion as Light and Dark: a theme only supplies colours.
  • Green still means tracking, amber a break and red overtime or a spent budget, in every theme. The accent is kept at least 40 degrees of hue away from all three.
  • Each theme carries its own six account colours (new: today they are one shared set), tuned so the dark-on-tint block text reads at 4.8:1 or better.
  • The pill keeps its soft tiles. On dark themes its ring and glow use the theme's own status colours, on light ones the brighter mint, amber and coral.
  • Contrast figures are worked out from the hex values shown, against the rules the app already tests: Ink 7:1, Muted 3.5:1, accent text 4.5:1.
