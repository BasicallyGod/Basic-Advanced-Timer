What is shared. All four keep the hour and minute steppers, the month and the footer exactly as they are; only where the four preset times sit changes. The same picker is used by reminders on notes, a meeting's When on the Notes page and rescheduling from the bell, so whichever is chosen applies to all three. A meeting's day (Day only, no time) is already just the month and the footer, 244 wide. The grey corners. The square grey corners at the card's edges in the Windows screenshot are not part of this design: they were the card's shadow being clipped by its pop-up window, and are fixed in the build by giving the card room for its shadow, so the corners are clean in every option here.
