The pill

About 72 wide by 262 tall at 100 per cent. One vertical capsule shows the recording state, and a second blue one appears beside it only while a reminder is due. The circle shows the running account's initials, and pause or resume when the pointer is over it. The amber mug under it starts a break, and while on a break it stays lit and steams, so the pill never shows two cups. There is no Finish day button and no open button: clicking the time opens the dashboard.
