The pill

About 72 wide by 300 tall at 100 per cent. One vertical capsule shows the recording state, and a second blue one appears beside it only while a reminder is due. The rounded square shows the running account's initials, and pause or resume when the pointer is over it. The three bars open the accounts, and the orange square at the foot starts a break. A soft ring and halo show the state without pulling the eye. There is no Finish day button: clicking the time opens the dashboard.
