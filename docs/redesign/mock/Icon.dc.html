Application icon

One drawing in one colour: the pill as a line outline, with its two lights (the state light and the reminder light) and a stack of bars for the hours, minutes and seconds, like the mark on the Getting started card. The icon is a white tile with the line in near black, with no gradient and no blue. A dark tile with a white line is the version for dark surfaces, and without a tile the line takes the colour of whatever it sits on, which is how the tray draws it. It replaces the green clock in the taskbar, the title bars, the tray, the installer and the Store tiles. Small sizes drop the seconds bar, thicken the outline and enlarge the lights so they still read. The lights are short and sit close together at the top, as on the pill, so the mark reads as a clock column and not a face or a pause symbol.

256 pixel master. Hover it: the lights show their state and reminder colours.
