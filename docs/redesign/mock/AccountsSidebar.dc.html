What has to be true for this to work
QuestionAnswer
Which window animates?The dashboard's. It is shown at full size with its content clipped to a rounded rectangle that grows from the pill's card, so no window is resized during the move (the rule that a visible window is never resized stays). The pill window hides when the clip starts and a copy of the pill, drawn inside the dashboard window, takes its place for the first 40 per cent.
Where does the dashboard open?With the sidebar's top left over the pill's card (today's PlaceByCard, now with the card lined up to the sidebar's top left). If the 1272 wide window would run off the screen it is placed lined up anyway, behind the clip, and its position eases over the same 340 ms to the nearest place that fits, so the pill appears to grow and drift in.
Where does the pill go back to?The top left of the sidebar, wherever the dashboard now is. Pressing the clock closes it; the pill then lives there.
What about the lights and the clock?The pill's clock is stacked (hours, minutes, seconds); the sidebar's is one line. They cross-fade while the card grows, rather than trying to morph digits, and the day clock keeps running underneath, so no second is ever skipped. The two lights are in the same place at the top of the card, so they look like one.
Reduced motionUnder Windows' Show animations off, m jumps from 0 to 1 and back (Motion.Ease is zero): the pill hides and the dashboard shows in one frame, with no clip.
Keyboard and focusOpening puts the focus on the sidebar's clock (where the pointer pressed on the pill) and closing puts it on the pill; neither takes focus from a box being typed in.
Dragging the pillA drag moves the pill window only, as today. The dashboard is not on screen while the pill is, so there is nothing to follow it.
CostOne animated clip geometry on the window's root and two opacities, for 340 ms. Nothing in the page lays out during it, because the page is at its final size throughout.
Other options weighedA plain cross-fade of the two windows (cheapest, but nothing connects the two shapes; it stays as the reduced-motion path). The pill staying put with the dashboard unfolding behind it, like the account drawer does today (that worked when the rail was the same 72 wide as the pill; now the sidebar's top is 248 wide and centred, so the pill would sit off to one side of it). Resizing one window (rejected: it flickers, and it breaks the rule above).
