Round 2: from the first Windows test

Five things to settle before more code: how accounts open from the pill and the rail (notes 1 and 5), the break button (2 and 4), the accounts icon (7), and the pill and rail matching in size (6). The circle no longer grows under the pointer (3): it shifts colour instead, which is already built. Decided and built. The Pill and Account drawer boards now show the result. Two things changed after testing and are not drawn below: the play button, the account rows' play buttons and the break are rounded squares rather than circles, and the break is the theme's orange (a plain orange cup on the rail), not the deeper amber.

1. Accounts, part of the pill

Recommended: A

The pill gains an Accounts button under the circle, the same one the rail has, so the top of the pill and the top of the rail are the same thing. Pressing it unfolds the list sideways from the pill, joined to it as one capsule. Pressing it again, pressing Esc or choosing an account folds it away. Nothing opens on hover, so no tooltip fights it. The pill is about 36 taller (300 instead of 262).

3. Break: an amber circle with a white cup

Drawn like the play circle, a little smaller (40 against 46), so it no longer lights up as a rounded square. The amber is deepened a little (about #B96F00) so the white cup holds 4.5 to 1. Hover brightens it, and on a break it carries a soft ring while the steam moves. The rail's foot then has one family of icons: the same 20 px box and 1.8 stroke for the flag and the cross (today the cup is a heavier drawing than they are).

4. No jump when the pill opens

Built in code, not drawn here

Today the pill is drawn at 70 to 85 per cent and the rail at 100, so the clock and circle jump when one becomes the other. The proposal: the pill is drawn a little larger (80 to 95 per cent by screen), and the rail's top and width follow the pill's size exactly, so the clock, the circle and the Accounts button stay put and the same size when the dashboard opens. The workspace to the right stays at 100 per cent, so its text is unchanged. Below, the pill and the rail's top at the same size, with guides at the same heights.
