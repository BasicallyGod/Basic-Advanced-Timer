The two lights at the top of the pill and the rail look too much like window buttons. Here are six shapes, each over the five states. The first row is today. The state is also in the circle's colour and glow, so the light never carries it alone.

What I would pick
Row three. One vertical capsule says what the timer is doing. A second blue one appears only when a reminder is due, and pulses. Most of the time there is one quiet shape, nothing like a row of three coloured dots, and the pill stays calmer.
Other things worth knowing
The same lights go on the dashboard rail. In Dark the capsules keep their colours. For people who cannot tell the colours apart, the circle already shows the account initials, pause or resume and the amber ring, and the tooltip names the state.
