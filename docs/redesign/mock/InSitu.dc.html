2The stack of blocks pressed

The tile takes the selection ring. The Selected block card shows the stack as a mini timeline at about three and a half times the timeline's scale, so each block is a readable bar. Pressing a bar picks that block: its line lifts in the tile, its bar is ringed with edge handles you can drag at 5px a minute, and the boxes below edit it as they do today.

  1. The tile is selected as a whole; the chosen block's line is the only one at full strength.
  2. The mini timeline replaces the overlap row, from the first start to the last end, with every block in its own row. No folded or dashed state exists any more.
  3. The boxes are the existing Account, Start, End, Length and Entry note for the ringed block. A single block or a two-deep overlap still uses the card exactly as it is today.

Things to decide
  1. When does a tile appear? I have drawn it for three or more at the same moment, and kept two overlapping blocks as two lanes as now (they read fine). The alternatives are any overlap, or four or more.
  2. Does zooming in split a tile back into lanes? It could, once the stack's bars would be at least 22px tall (around 400px an hour). I'd leave it a tile at every zoom, so the day never rearranges itself.
  3. Dragging a pile's edges on the timeline is lost; the mini timeline's handles (and the boxes) replace it. Moving a block clear of the pile is done with its Start and End boxes.
  4. Blocks being recorded that overlap a pile would stay drawn on their own lane above the tile, since the live block is the one thing you watch.
