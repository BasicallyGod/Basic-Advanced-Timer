Tooltips and rail icons

Two things to decide before they are built. Tooltips say too much and look like the computer's, not the app's. And the Notes button on the rail sits under the Timeline button with an icon that reads much the same.

Tooltips, now and proposed
The same controls. The left tip is the computer's own box with a sentence in it. The right one is the app's card: panel colour, a hairline, a soft shadow, the action in a word or two, and the shortcut as a small key.

The rules
A button with words needs no tip. Add time, Undo and Copy already say what they do. The tip stays only to show a shortcut.
An icon gets its action in one to three words. Copy, Delete, Zoom out, Collapse.
Facts are lines, never sentences. A day, a budget or a bar shows its figures one to a line. The budget keeps its pace as "On pace".
One card everywhere. The same as menus and pop-ups, in both themes, with no arrow. It appears after about half a second and goes at once.

Rail icons, as chosen, with motion
Timeline bars, Week columns and the solid ticked box for Notes, with the tick lifted so it sits in the middle of its box and the second box now solid. Hover a rail icon to play it. The three large ones play all the time so they can be looked at. With "Show animations" turned off in Windows they stay still.

Timeline
The three bars slide a little left and right, each at its own slow pace (2.2, 2.4 and 2.7 seconds, close to the columns' 2.6), so they never move together and the loop has no seam.
Week overview
The columns spring up from the line one after another, each a beat behind the last, then settle. It repeats every 2.6 seconds.
Notes
The tick draws itself in the top box and holds, then fades as the X draws itself in the bottom box. They take turns every 4.4 seconds.
